Pub Games Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Pub Games is a local party-game app. We respect your privacy and do not operate accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or a Pub Games cloud server.

Information processed by the app

  • Player names: Names entered for multiplayer games are stored locally on your device and shared with participating nearby devices during the game.
  • Game content: Custom questions, decks, preferences, and scores are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to us.
  • Photos: Photo Shuffle can request access to your photo library. Photos are selected and processed on your device. If you tap “Share Photos & Ready,” selected photos are sent directly to the nearby devices participating in that game. They are not uploaded to a Pub Games server.
  • Motion data: The currently hidden Who Am I game may use device motion only to detect tilt controls. Motion data is processed on the device and is not collected or transmitted to us.

Multiplayer connections

Multiplayer uses Apple’s Network framework to discover and connect to nearby devices. Pub Games does not use Multipeer Connectivity, remote matchmaking, cloud gameplay servers, or a Pub Games backend.

During a multiplayer session, temporary gameplay information such as display names, player identifiers, answers, scores, game state, and optional Photo Shuffle submissions may be transmitted directly between participating devices.

The app’s multiplayer transport does not provide app-level TLS or end-to-end encryption. Only join tables with people you trust.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect, sell, or share personal information for advertising or tracking. The app does not collect:

  • Accounts, email addresses, or contact information
  • Location data or contacts
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Analytics or browsing history
  • Photos uploaded to a server

Storage and retention

Player names, custom content, and preferences remain on your device unless you remove them or delete the app. Active multiplayer data and Photo Shuffle images are kept temporarily for the current game and are cleared when the session ends or you leave the game. Other players may still independently save or capture content displayed on their own devices.

Third-party services

Pub Games does not use third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, cloud-storage, or matchmaking services. The app uses Apple system frameworks, including Network, Photos, and Core Motion, only to provide the features described above.

Children’s privacy

Pub Games does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app’s features or data practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
